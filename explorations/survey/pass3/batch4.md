Cross-cutting finding. Two warehouse parquets hold a Hub "download pending" stub, not data. ## Top 3 findings

1. **Two warehouse "empty" tables are an ingester bug, not data loss.** The Hub returns a `{"status":"Pending"}` stub on the first CSV download, and the ingester saved it as a parquet (`open_theft_of_motor_vehicle`, `ork_pfas_...`). The source data is fine: 11,720 theft rows and 31 PFAS rows (up from 23, after an edit on 2026-09-22). The same fault likely explains `ops_traffic_stops_open_data` (rows 0, no parquet file). That table is 455,717 rows live and includes `driver_race`.
2. **The OPS portal is far bigger than catalogued and not in the warehouse.** It has 98 DCAT items (not 11 datasets), including Criminal Offences (344,290 rows), Dispatched Calls For Service (1.11 M rows, 2021-2025, edited 2026-09-29 despite "annual, Feb 2026") and Traffic Stops. `crime-map`'s catalogued open.ottawa mirror is stale (2024-10-03, `{{description}}` template, app only, no bulk). The real live layer is `CrimeMap_YTD/FeatureServer/8` (30,349 rows, edited today).
3. **Liveness is unreliable for `ottawa.ca/en/...` and `ottawapublichealth.ca`.** `housing-homelessness`, `drinking-water` and `solid-waste` return 200 with an Incapsula challenge page (about 1 KB, no title), so stage-0 "live" means nothing for them. The catalogued OPH URL has silently died (404 since after 2026-09-09) after a site restructure to `/statistics-and-reports/`. And `fire-paramedic` promises station locations and response times that the portal does not hold (only 8 annual fire counts to 2022 and paramedic daily volumes to 2023).
