### howtheyvoted
**LIVE.** Access tag `api, html`: matches (static JSON files, not a queryable API).
- **verified:** `https://howtheyvoted.ca/data/ottawa/index.json` returned 200 `application/json` (12 KB). It has keys `dates` (490 entries, 2026-10-08 down to 2022-11-28) and `councillors` (25). `/data/ottawa/dates/2026-10-08.json` returned 200 (39 KB) and holds City Council meeting #87 with attendance and agenda_items. Warehouse `htv_meetings` covers 2022-11-28 to 2026-10-08, with 490 distinct dates and 588 meetings. That matches the live index exactly, so the warehouse is current.
- **anomaly (verified, important for analysis):** individual votes exist for only a small share of motions. Warehouse `htv_motions` has 7,351 rows, but `vote_kind` is `none` for 7,022, `recorded` for 328 and `dissent` for 1. `htv_votes` (6,169 rows: 3,875 for, 2,294 against) covers only 329 distinct `motion_id`s. The description "council voting-record site" overstates what can be analysed per councillor.
- **unique (verified):** `htv_attendance` has 5,871 rows of per-meeting attendance, with start times and locations, machine-readable from the eSCRIBE minutes.
- **anomaly (candidate):** the term is nearly over (election 2026-10-26). The latest meeting in the data is 2026-10-08, so the term file is effectively final.

## Three most interesting findings

1. **howtheyvoted covers recorded votes for only about 4.5% of motions.** Of 7,351 motions, 328 are `recorded` plus 1 `dissent`, and `htv_votes` has named votes for 329 motions. Per-councillor analysis rests on that thin slice. The warehouse is current to 2026-10-08, and the OttWatch meeting GUID equals the HTV `meeting_id`, giving a clean join key.
2. **OttWatch recovered and has an RSS feed.** All three probed paths return 200 (the v1 host is still absent), the newest announcement is 2026-10-14, and `?format=rss` gives 10 items. The 2026-09-15 `verify_note` can be retired, and the `html` tag is understated.
3. **Several "200" liveness results hide broken or stale content.** ottawa.ca (city-archives) is an Incapsula wall, including its Wayback copy. The data.ontario FIR datastore holds `<!DOCTYPE html>` rather than data, and its two warehouse manifest entries have no parquet files. The ACORN scorecard is from October 2022, and the Lookout "tracker" URL is a May 2026 news post.
