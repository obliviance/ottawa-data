## Three most interesting findings
1. **ASE cameras were switched off on 2025-11-13 (Bill 56)**; the catalogue description is silent, the traffic.ottawa.ca `ase_camera` endpoint still lists 58 sites (undocumented, different ids/coordinates from the Open Ottawa 60-row table), and a monthly "Removal - Monitoring Speed Data" table gives a pre/active/removed speed series at 8 pilot sites, updated to 2026-10-09.
2. **Wrong/understated surfaces for planning data**: the zoning URL in sources.json points at the retired 2008-250 service (the 2026-50 service is `Zoning_Bylaw_2026_50`, with a 381-polygon "under appeal" layer joined to OLT case `OLT-26-000364`); Official Plan has a keyless 89-layer MapServer; development applications have a keyless MapServer of 3,455 files (vs 775 in the keyed REST API) with application and status dates (one status date is in the future, 2026-11-23); building permits are published per permit (Jan-Aug 2026: about 6,290 rows) with cancellations/old-dated rows appearing from April 2026.
3. **Delay-capable live feeds exist but nothing is archived**: traffic.ottawa.ca events v2 (planned schedule start/end plus `updated` for 129 live closures), static GTFS (rolling one-month window, nightly) with keyed GTFS-RT, devapps `endOfCirculationDate` and status dates, OLT case status (two-year retention). All are current-state only; any planned-vs-actual analysis requires scheduled snapshotting. Also: collisions have no 2023 and no 2025 data, and every ottawa.ca page returns a 200 Imperva wall that verification.json mislabels as "spa".
